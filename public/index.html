THE ROOTS OF THIS MAP

Open tools.
Traceable data.

This independent recreation uses Leaflet and locally stored data. It needs no ArcGIS account.

The bike network

Bike Streets supplies the community-selected low-stress network and construction disruptions. The OSM-marked bikeways in “Designated bike routes (more traffic)” are retrieved from the designated layer of Bike Streets’ public tiles. This bounded snapshot covers the urban area through New Orleans East; it is not a complete parish inventory.

City bikeways

The City of New Orleans public bikeway layer includes only facilities marked as existing. Proposed-only facilities are excluded. Its reported last data edit is February 18, 2025. The download is stored locally and uses the same canopy calculation. It appears together with OSM-marked bikeways under “Designated bike routes (more traffic),” using the same dotted canopy colors. This display grouping does not assign a traffic-stress rating to city facilities; their facility types remain in the popups. Each display layer has separate mileage and length-weighted canopy statistics, following its visibility and canopy filter. Designated bike routes (more traffic) combines OSM and city source totals; overlapping linework may be counted twice. Unknown canopy is excluded from the average. These are mapped source lengths, not deduplicated street miles.

Best-covered stretches

These 1 km connected candidates from each enabled network are ranked by length-weighted nearby canopy. A bounded graph search finds strong candidates rather than an exhaustive mathematical optimum. Construction-marked segments are excluded, and picks sharing more than 25% of their source length are removed. The source does not supply the turn restrictions needed for turn-by-turn directions.

What the canopy percentage means

The analysis counts tree-canopy pixels within 10 meters of each route segment (up to 100 meters long), using NOAA’s 1-meter land-cover classification. At least 90% of a buffer must have valid data to receive a score. Unknown coverage is never shown as zero.

NOAA C-CAP imagery is from 2017, before Hurricane Ida and later tree loss or planting. Canopy near a street does not measure shade at a particular time of day. Ground-check routes and conditions.

A new analysis, with the same inspiration

Bike Easy’s original map included Laura Harris’s hand-selected shaded corridors and pink connectors. Those choices have not been recovered or recreated here. Colors on this map represent calculated canopy coverage when available.
